# AI Automation Case Studies | AI Native

**Live Demo:** https://aiautomationcasestudies.netlify.app/

## Overview

AI Automation Case Studies is an AI Native portfolio project exploring practical applications of artificial intelligence and workflow automation across business processes.

The project presents automation use cases to demonstrate how AI-assisted workflows can help structure repetitive tasks, connect processes, improve operational efficiency, and support smarter decision-making.

## Project Objectives

- Explore practical AI automation use cases.
- Demonstrate workflow design and process optimization.
- Examine how AI can support repetitive business and marketing tasks.
- Communicate automation concepts through structured case studies.
- Explore opportunities for responsible, human-supervised automation.

## Areas of Exploration

- **Workflow Automation:** Structuring repeatable processes and connecting tasks.
- **AI-Assisted Operations:** Exploring the application of AI to information processing and routine workflows.
- **Process Optimization:** Identifying opportunities to reduce unnecessary manual work.
- **Marketing Automation:** Exploring how automation can support lead management, email workflows, and marketing operations.
- **Responsible Automation:** Considering human oversight, data privacy, and output validation.
